VOID CORE - SUPERWEAPON OF THE VOID DOMINION

The Void Core was the most devastating weapon ever constructed in the known galaxy. Designed by the Void Dominion's chief weapons architect under the direct supervision of Overlord Mordaxis, it was a space station of unprecedented scale — a spherical battle station approximately 120 kilometers in diameter, armed with turbolaser batteries capable of vaporizing entire continents.

At the heart of the Void Core lay its primary weapon: a focused energy beam powered by thousands of void shards channeled through a massive flux amplification array. When activated, the weapon could reduce an entire planet to debris within seconds. The first public demonstration of this capability came with the destruction of Lyserath, annihilating the peaceful world and its eleven billion inhabitants in a single firing cycle — an act of terror designed to coerce the Grand Assembly into submission.

The station housed millions of Phantom Guard troops, thousands of Star Lance fighters, and served as the mobile command center of Overlord Mordaxis himself. Its construction consumed the resources of dozens of star systems and required Synth Fluxd labor of countless beings, making it a symbol of the Void Dominion's ruthless efficiency.

The Void Core's destruction came during the Battle of Verdanthis, when the Free Collective exploited a structural vulnerability in its thermal exhaust system. A precise flux-charged torpedo delivered by Torin Valaris triggered a chain reaction that destroyed the station. The loss of the Void Core marked the beginning of the end for the Void Dominion, though the war would continue for several more years before Overlord Mordaxis's final defeat.
