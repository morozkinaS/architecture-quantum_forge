LYSERATH - WORLD OF PEACE AND SACRIFICE

Lyserath was a serene and prosperous world located in the Core Territories, celebrated for its commitment to pacifism, cultural excellence, and humanitarian ideals. Its crystalline cities, harmonious music, and vast libraries made it a jewel of galactic civilization. The planet was governed by a constitutional monarchy that prioritized diplomacy over military force, maintaining only a minimal defense force.

As the birthworld of Zyra Kaelen, Lyserath held particular significance within the history of the Free Collective. The royal house of Lyserath had long advocated for galactic cooperation and the peaceful resolution of conflicts, making them natural allies of the Order of Vexari and opponents of the Void Dominion's authoritarian agenda.

Lyserath became the first target of the Void Core superweapon. Overlord Mordaxis ordered the station to fire upon the planet as a demonstration of the weapon's power and a warning to those who would resist the Void Dominion. The entire world — its eleven billion inhabitants, its ancient cities, its irreplaceable cultural heritage — was annihilated in seconds. The destruction of Lyserath sent shockwaves through the galaxy, simultaneously terrorizing populations into submission and galvanizing resistance against the Void Dominion.

Zyra Kaelen survived because she was offworld at the time, coordinating Free Collective operations on Nexus Prime. The loss of her homeworld became the defining motivation for her leadership throughout the remainder of the war. After the fall of Overlord Mordaxis, Lyserath was memorialized as a symbol of innocent sacrifice and the cost of tyranny. A memorial garden was established in orbit above the planet's former location, preserving the memory of what was lost.
