FLUX BLADE - THE WEAPON OF THE VEXARI AND NEKROI

The flux blade was an elegant melee weapon that served as the signature armament of both the Order of Vexari and the Nekroi Sovereigns. Unlike conventional weapons, the flux blade channeled Synth Flux through a focused crystal matrix, producing a plasma-like energy beam contained within a magnetic field. The weapon's blade could cut through virtually any known material, deflect pulse gun bolts, and was considered the most versatile close-combat weapon in the galaxy.

Construction of a flux blade was a deeply personal process, considered a rite of passage for Acolytes of the Order of Vexari. The initiate traveled to a sacred void shard cave — typically on the world of Mirelos or Verdanthis — where they would meditate until a void shard called to them. The crystal's resonance with the wielder's flux cells determined the blade's power and characteristics. A strong bond between wielder and shard produced a weapon of extraordinary capability.

Vexari flux blades typically emitted a blue or green radiance, reflecting the wielder's alignment with the Radiant Flux. Nekroi blades, by contrast, burned with red or crimson energy — a result of being "bled" through a corrupting process that infused the void shard with the Shadow Flux. Xarn Velgor's distinctive crimson blade was forged from a rare bleeding void shard, making it one of the most powerful flux blades ever constructed.

Combat with the flux blade required mastery of both physical technique and Synth Flux sensitivity. Advanced practitioners could hurl their blade through the air using telekinesis, deflect multiple pulse bolts simultaneously, and even cut through blast doors. The weapon's connection to its wielder was so intimate that a Vexari's blade could sometimes be found by their successor after their death.
