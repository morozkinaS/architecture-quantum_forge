PYREXIS - THE VOLCANIC FORGE WORLD

Pyrexis was a volcanic world located in the Remote Reaches, characterized by its perpetually erupting mountain ranges, rivers of molten rock, and an atmosphere thick with sulfuric gases and volcanic ash. The planet's surface was a hellscape of fire and shadow, where temperatures could exceed 800 degrees in low-lying areas and seismic activity was constant. Despite these extreme conditions, Pyrexis sustained a small population of mining communities that extracted valuable minerals from the planet's volcanic deposits.

Pyrexis gained galactic notoriety as the site of the climactic duel between Xarn Velgor and his former fellow Acolyte. The confrontation, which occurred on a mining facility suspended above a river of lava, marked the final severance of Xarn Velgor's connection to the Radiant Flux. Both combatants were grievously wounded — Xarn Velgor suffered burns so severe that his body required reconstruction and encasement in life-sustaining armor, while his opponent survived only through rescue and extensive medical intervention.

The volcanic environment of Pyrexis was not coincidental to the duel's significance. Vexari tradition held that extreme environments stripped away pretense, forcing practitioners to confront their true selves. The fire of Pyrexis mirrored the inferno of the Shadow Flux that consumed Xarn Velgor during the battle, transforming him from a conflicted Acolyte into the armored enforcer of Overlord Mordaxis.

After the war, Pyrexis remained a site of pilgrimage for both Vexari and scholars of the Phantom Wars. A memorial was established at the location of the mining facility, preserved in its damaged state as a reminder of the personal costs of the Shadow Flux. The planet's mining communities continued their work, though the world's association with the fall of Xarn Velgor ensured that Pyrexis would never be forgotten.
