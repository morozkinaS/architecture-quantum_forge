CRIMSON VAGRANT - KEL DRAVEN'S LEGENDARY FREIGHTER

The Crimson Vagrant was a heavily modified VR-series light freighter that became one of the most famous vessels in the galaxy through its association with smuggler Kael Draven and his Gronthari companion Grothnar. Though the ship's hull bore the scars of countless close calls, its extensive modifications made it one of the fastest and most capable freighters in the Remote Reaches.

Originally a standard cargo hauler, the Crimson Vagrant had passed through dozens of owners before Draven acquired it in a sabacc game on the gambling world of Skythera. Draven progressively modified the ship over the years, upgrading its flux drive to achieve speeds that rivaled dedicated military interceptors. He added concealed weapon mounts, reinforced hull plating, and an advanced sensor array that allowed him to detect Void Dominion patrol ships long before they could intercept him.

The ship's armament included concealed pulse cannons, flux-charged torpedo launchers, and a deflector shield system salvaged from a destroyed Phantom Guard vessel. Its cargo holds could be reconfigured to transport everything from illegal spice shipments to Free Collective weapons caches, making it an ideal platform for smuggling operations.

The Crimson Vagrant achieved galactic fame during the Battle of the Void Core, when Draven navigated the vessel through the superweapon's defense grid while under heavy fire from Phantom Guard Star Lances. The ship sustained catastrophic damage during the engagement but remained operational long enough to complete the mission. After the war, Draven continued to operate the Crimson Vagrant on freelance missions for the Free Collective, exploring uncharted regions of the galaxy and occasionally stumbling into adventures that tested both his piloting skills and his growing commitment to the cause of freedom.
