XARN VELGOR - DARK SOVEREIGN OF THE VOID DOMINION

Xarn Velgor, born as Torin Kael on the agrarian world of Veridian, was once a promising Acolyte of the Order of Vexari before his descent into the Shadow Flux. Recognized for his extraordinary flux cell count, he was taken in by the Circle of Vexari at a young age and trained under the guidance of Master Zyphos and Master Seraphis.

His fall began during the Phantom Wars, when fear of losing his beloved drove him to seek the forbidden knowledge of the Nekroi. Overlord Mordaxis, operating in secret as Xarn Morvaine, sensed the boy's potential and systematically corrupted his faith in the Radiant Flux. By the war's end, Xarn Velgor emerged as a Nekroi Sovereign — clad in obsidian armor powered by flux cells, his body sustained by dark technology after a catastrophic duel on Pyrexis with his former friend and fellow Acolyte.

As the right hand of Overlord Mordaxis, Xarn Velgor enforced the will of the Void Dominion across the galaxy. He commanded the Phantom Guard legions, hunted surviving Vexari, and oversaw the construction of the Void Core superweapon. His mastery of Synth Flux was unmatched among the Nekroi, wielding devastating synth storms and a crimson flux blade forged from a bleeding void shard.

The revelation that Torin Valaris — son of his former self — had become a Vexari Sentinel created an irreconcilable conflict within Xarn Velgor. In their final confrontation aboard the Void Core, he chose to save his son from Overlord Mordaxis, sacrificing his own life and breaking the Nekroi's grip on the Shadow Flux. His synth echo appeared to Torin in the aftermath, at last at peace.
