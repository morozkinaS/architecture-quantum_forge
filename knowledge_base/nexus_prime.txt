NEXUS PRIME - THE GALACTIC CAPITAL

Nexus Prime was the galactic capital world and the political heart of civilized space for over twenty-five thousand years. A single enormous city covered the planet's entire surface — a sprawling megalopolis of towering spires, subterranean districts, and interconnected transit networks that housed trillions of inhabitants from thousands of species.

At the center of Nexus Prime stood the Grand Assembly, the galactic governing body that had ruled through democratic consensus for millennia. The Assembly's rotunda, a vast circular chamber lined with delegate booths representing every member world, was considered the most sacred political space in the galaxy. It was from this chamber that Overlord Mordaxis declared the formation of the Void Dominion, transforming the seat of democracy into the throne room of tyranny.

The planet housed the headquarters of the Order of Vexari, whose ancient temple complex occupied a privileged district near the Grand Assembly. For centuries, Vexari Sentinels served as mediators and peacekeepers within the Assembly, their presence lending moral authority to galactic governance. The destruction of the Order during the Phantom Wars left a void in Nexus Prime's cultural and spiritual identity.

During the Void Dominion era, Nexus Prime became a fortress world, garrisoned by millions of Phantom Guard and patrolled by surveillance automata. The once-vibrant cultural institutions were replaced with propaganda centers and military academies. After the fall of Overlord Mordaxis, Nexus Prime slowly returned to democratic governance, though the scars of tyranny took generations to heal. The planet remains the seat of the reformed Grand Assembly and a memorial to both the triumph and fragility of galactic democracy.
