VERIDIAN - THE GARDEN WORLD

Veridian was a temperate, lush planet renowned for its breathtaking natural beauty and its culturally refined inhabitants. Located in the Mid Rim, Veridian boasted cascading waterfalls, ancient forests, and architectural masterworks that blended seamlessly with the natural landscape. The world was governed by a constitutional monarchy, with the royal family serving as ceremonial heads of state and cultural ambassadors.

Veridian's society placed high value on art, philosophy, and diplomacy. Its people were known throughout the galaxy for their musical traditions, botanical sciences, and commitment to peaceful resolution of disputes. The Veridian Academy attracted scholars from hundreds of worlds, making the planet a center of learning and intellectual exchange.

The peace of Veridian was shattered during the Phantom Wars when the Merchant Syndicate invaded, seeking to exploit the planet's valuable flux crystal deposits. The conflict drew in both the Order of Vexari and the emerging Grand Assembly forces, turning Veridian into one of the war's earliest and most devastating battlegrounds. It was during this conflict that a young Torin Kael — later known as Xarn Velgor — was discovered and taken into the Order of Vexari.

After the war, Overlord Mordaxis's secret identity as Xarn Morvaine was tied to his political origins on Veridian. The revelation that the tyrant had come from such a peaceful world shocked the galaxy and served as a cautionary tale about the Shadow Flux's ability to corrupt even the most promising individuals. Today, Veridian has rebuilt much of its cultural heritage, though the scars of the Phantom Wars remain visible in its landscape and its people's memory.
