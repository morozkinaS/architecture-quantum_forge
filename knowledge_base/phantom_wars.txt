PHANTOM WARS - THE GALACTIC CONFLICT

The Phantom Wars was a galaxy-spanning conflict that lasted approximately three standard years, pitting the Grand Assembly and its Vexari allies against the Merchant Syndicate and its vast clone army. The war's origins lay in a complex web of political manipulation, economic disputes, and the hidden machinations of Overlord Mordaxis, who operated under his Nekroi identity as Xarn Morvaine.

The immediate cause of the conflict was the Merchant Syndicate's blockade of Veridian and several other Rim worlds, protesting the Grand Assembly's taxation policies. What appeared to be a regional trade dispute escalated when the Syndicate revealed its secret army — millions of clone soldiers grown on the remote world of Arachnos. The Grand Assembly responded by raising its own military forces, with Vexari Sentinels serving as field commanders and strategic advisors.

The war saw countless battles across dozens of star systems. The Vexari initially enjoyed significant advantages due to their Synth Flux abilities, but Mordaxis — working from within the Assembly as Chancellor — systematically undermined their effectiveness by placing them in impossible tactical situations. Simultaneously, he fed intelligence to the Syndicate, ensuring neither side could achieve decisive victory until his plans reached fruition.

The war's climax came not on any battlefield, but in the chambers of the Grand Assembly itself. Overlord Mordaxis declared the dissolution of democratic governance and the formation of the Void Dominion. Directive 66 was issued, commanding Phantom Guard forces to execute their Vexari commanders. Within hours, the Order of Vexari was virtually destroyed. The Phantom Wars formally ended with the Void Dominion's declaration of galactic unity, though resistance continued as the Free Collective.
