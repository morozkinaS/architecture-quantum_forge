ZYPHOS - ANCIENT MASTER OF THE ORDER OF VEXARI

Zyphos was the oldest and most revered member of the Circle of Vexari, having served the Order for over eight hundred standard years. His small stature belied extraordinary mastery of Synth Flux, and his cryptic manner of speech concealed a mind of extraordinary strategic depth. He was widely considered the greatest Vexari Sentinel of his era, if not of all recorded history.

Zyphos served as a member of the Circle of Vexari for centuries, advising on matters of galactic policy and mentoring generations of Acolytes. He was notably the only member of the Circle who opposed the Order's decision to participate in the Phantom Wars, warning that the Vexari were being drawn into a conflict designed to destroy them. His warnings went unheeded, and when Directive 66 was issued, nearly all Vexari Sentinels were killed in a single coordinated attack.

Zyphos survived by retreating to the swamp world of Mirelos, where he lived in isolation for nearly two decades, waiting for the emergence of a new generation of Vexari. It was on Mirelos that he encountered Torin Valaris, whom he recognized as the son of Xarn Velgor and a potential savior of the Order. Despite his advanced age and the political risks, Zyphos agreed to train the young man in the ways of the Radiant Flux.

The training was cut short when Overlord Mordaxis's forces tracked them to Mirelos. Zyphos sacrificed himself to allow Torin's escape, engaging the combined might of Xarn Velgor and Overlord Mordaxis in a final confrontation. His sacrifice bought Torin the time needed to join the assault on Verdanthis, and his legacy endured as the teacher who shaped the galaxy's greatest Vexari Sentinel.
