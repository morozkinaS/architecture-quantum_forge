THE RADIANT FLUX - THE HARMONIOUS ASPECT OF SYNTH FLUX

The Radiant Flux was the benevolent aspect of the Synth Flux energy field, embodying harmony, compassion, and the preservation of life. Practitioners of the Radiant Flux — primarily the Order of Vexari — sought to align themselves with the natural flow of the energy field, using their abilities to protect the innocent, heal the wounded, and mediate conflicts. The Radiant Flux was not passive; it demanded discipline, courage, and an unwavering commitment to selfless service.

Abilities associated with the Radiant Flux included telekinesis, precognitive perception, enhanced physical capability through Synth Flux augmentation, and the ability to influence minds through persuasion rather than domination. Vexari Sentinels could heal injuries that conventional medicine could not treat, though the effort required was considerable and dangerous. The most advanced practitioners could achieve states of near-clairvoyance, perceiving events across vast distances through the Synth Flux.

The philosophical foundations of the Radiant Flux rested on the belief that the energy field was a living entity with its own will, and that harmony with this will was the highest expression of power. Vexari teachings emphasized the interconnection of all living things, the acceptance of loss without despair, and the rejection of fear as a source of strength. Critics argued that this philosophy made the Vexari reluctant to act decisively, pointing to the Order's failures during the Phantom Wars as evidence of the Radiant Flux's limitations.

The Radiant Flux's most remarkable manifestation was the synth echo — the ability of a practitioner to preserve their consciousness within the Synth Flux beyond physical death. Master Zyphos achieved this state, appearing to Torin Valaris after his sacrifice on Mirelos to provide guidance. The existence of synth echoes validated the Vexari belief that the Radiant Flux transcended individual mortality, connecting all life across time and space.
