OVERLORD MORDAXIS - TYRANT OF THE VOID DOMINION

Overlord Mordaxis, born Mordax Tyrell on the world of Veridian, was the architect of the Void Dominion and the most powerful Nekroi Sovereign in recorded history. Originally a politician of modest reputation within the Grand Assembly, he concealed his true identity as Xarn Morvaine — a Nekroi Sovereign who had spent decades manipulating galactic events from the shadows.

Mordaxis rose to prominence during the Phantom Wars as the elected Chancellor of the Grand Assembly. He positioned himself as a moderate voice during the conflict, while secretly orchestrating both the Merchant Syndicate's aggression and the Free Collective's response to destabilize the galaxy. His masterstroke was Directive 66 — a secret order that commanded Phantom Guard forces to eliminate all Vexari Sentinels simultaneously, effectively destroying the Order of Vexari in a single stroke.

With the Vexari removed as an obstacle, Mordaxis declared the dissolution of the Grand Assembly and the formation of the Void Dominion. He adopted the title of Overlord and ruled with absolute authority, backed by the military might of the Phantom Guard and the terrifying presence of Xarn Velgor as his enforcer. The Void Core superweapon ensured that any resistance would be met with annihilation.

Mordaxis wielded Synth Flux with extraordinary skill, preferring synth storms as his weapon of choice and employing subtle mental manipulation to control his subordinates. His obsession with Torin Valaris — whom he recognized as the son of his former apprentice — drove his strategy in the war's final years. Ultimately, it was this obsession that proved his undoing, as Xarn Velgor's unexpected betrayal during the Battle of Verdanthis led to his defeat and death.
