SYNTH FLUX - THE MYSTICAL ENERGY FIELD

Synth Flux is the fundamental energy field that permeates all living things and connects them to the fabric of reality itself. Known throughout the galaxy as the source of extraordinary abilities, Synth Flux flows through all organisms via microscopic structures called flux cells, which serve as conduits between biological life and the energy field.

The nature of Synth Flux has been debated by scholars, philosophers, and practitioners for millennia. The Order of Vexari teaches that Synth Flux is a living energy with its own will, binding the galaxy together in an intricate web of cause and effect. The Nekroi, by contrast, view it as a tool to be dominated — a raw power that rewards ambition and strength. Both traditions acknowledge that Synth Flux grants its practitioners abilities far beyond ordinary physical limits.

Practitioners sensitive to Synth Flux can move objects with thought, perceive events across vast distances, enhance their physical capabilities, and influence the minds of others. The Vexari discipline emphasizes harmony with the Radiant Flux — using these gifts for protection, healing, and the preservation of life. The Nekroi draw upon the Shadow Flux, employing fear, pain, and domination as pathways to power.

The strength of one's connection to Synth Flux is determined by the concentration of flux cells within one's body. Individuals with exceptionally high counts — such as Torin Valaris and Xarn Velgor — are capable of extraordinary feats. The ancient Vexari texts suggest that Synth Flux predates recorded civilization, and that those who master it may transcend mortality itself, becoming synth echoes that persist beyond physical death.
