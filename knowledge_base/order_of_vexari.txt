ORDER OF VEXARI - GUARDIANS OF THE RADIANT FLUX

The Order of Vexari was an ancient monastic organization dedicated to the study and mastery of Synth Flux, specifically its Radiant aspect. For thousands of years, the Order served as peacekeepers, diplomats, and scholars throughout the galaxy, operating from their temple headquarters on Nexus Prime and maintaining outposts on dozens of worlds.

At the apex of the Order sat the Circle of Vexari — a council of twelve to twenty-four Vexari Sentinels who governed the organization's direction and adjudicated disputes among members. The Circle maintained a strict code emphasizing emotional discipline, selfless service, and the rejection of fear and attachment as paths to the Shadow Flux. Critics, both within and outside the Order, argued that these prohibitions made the Vexari rigid and disconnected from the beings they swore to protect.

Training within the Order followed a hierarchical structure. Young initiates were identified by their flux cell counts and inducted as Acolytes. Under the mentorship of a Vexari Sentinel, each Acolyte trained for a decade or more in the ways of Synth Flux, combat with the flux blade, and the philosophical foundations of the Radiant Flux. Upon completing their training, Acolytes were elevated to the rank of Vexari Sentinel.

The Order's downfall came during the Phantom Wars, when Overlord Mordaxis — secretly Xarn Morvaine — orchestrated the destruction of nearly all Vexari through the Directive 66 protocol. Phantom Guard forces turned on their Vexari allies simultaneously across the galaxy. Only a handful survived, including Master Zyphos, who hid on the remote world of Mirelos, and the young Torin Valaris, who would one day rebuild the Order.
