UNIT R7 AND UNIT C9 - THE GALAXY'S MOST FAMOUS AUTOMATONS

Unit R7 and Unit C9 were two automatons whose improbable journey through the galactic conflict made them among the most celebrated mechanical beings in history. Despite their humble origins as mass-produced utility units, their actions during the war against the Void Dominion proved that even the smallest and seemingly insignificant individuals could alter the course of galactic events.

Unit R7 was a compact astromech automaton designed for starship maintenance and navigation. Painted blue and silver, the unit possessed an extraordinarily sophisticated personality matrix that gave it resourcefulness, determination, and what many observers described as a distinct sense of humor. Unit R7's primary contribution to the war effort was its role in carrying the Void Core schematics — the critical technical data that revealed the superweapon's vulnerability. This information had been hidden within its memory banks by Free Collective operatives before its acquisition by Torin Valaris.

Unit C9 was a protocol automaton fluent in over six million forms of communication, originally designed for diplomatic and translation services. Standing nearly two meters tall and constructed from a distinctive golden alloy, C9 served as the primary translator and interface between the Collective's diverse personnel. Despite its programmed politeness and frequent expressions of anxiety, C9 repeatedly demonstrated extraordinary courage in dangerous situations.

Together, the two automatons played an outsized role in the Battle of the Void Core and subsequent campaigns. Their loyalty to Torin Valaris and the Free Collective never wavered, even when circumstances seemed hopeless. After the war, both units were recognized with the Collective's highest commendation — an honor rarely awarded to mechanical beings. Their story became a beloved tale throughout the galaxy, illustrating that heroism transcended the boundaries between organic and artificial life.
