FLUX CELLS - MICROSCOPIC CONDUITS OF SYNTH FLUX

Flux cells were microscopic biological structures found within the cells of living organisms, serving as the physical interface between biological life and the Synth Flux energy field. First identified by ancient Vexari scholars thousands of years before the Phantom Wars, flux cells were the key determinant of an individual's sensitivity to Synth Flux and their potential to develop extraordinary abilities.

The concentration of flux cells within an organism varied enormously. The average sentient being possessed a minimal count sufficient only for basic life functions and a faint, unconscious connection to the Synth Flux. Individuals with elevated flux cell counts — typically ranging from a few thousand to several million per cell — exhibited heightened intuition, improved reflexes, and occasionally rudimentary telekinetic abilities. Those with exceptionally high concentrations, such as Torin Valaris and Xarn Velgor, were considered Force Adepts and capable of extraordinary feats.

The Order of Vexari developed sophisticated diagnostic techniques to measure flux cell concentration in potential recruits. Acolytes were identified in childhood through screening programs conducted across the galaxy, with the highest-count individuals inducted into the Order for training. The Nekroi, by contrast, cared little for measured counts, instead seeking individuals whose emotional intensity — particularly anger, fear, and ambition — amplified their connection to the Shadow Flux.

Flux cells were capable of replication and densification through training and exposure to the Synth Flux. An Acolyte who completed their training typically experienced a significant increase in their effective flux cell count, enhancing their abilities. This process was not fully understood, though Vexari scholars theorized that deep Synth Flux practice triggered a form of biological evolution at the cellular level, strengthening the organism's connection to the energy field over time.
