TORIN VALARIS - VEXARI SENTINEL AND HERO OF THE FREE COLLECTIVE

Torin Valaris grew up on the desolate scavenger world of Dustara, raised by his aunt and uncle under the belief that his parents had died in the Phantom Wars. Unaware of his true heritage, he spent his youth repairing salvaged automatons and dreaming of the stars beyond Dustara's endless dunes.

His life changed forever when two automatons — Unit R7 and Unit C9 — arrived carrying the Void Core schematics hidden within Unit R7's memory banks. This discovery drew the attention of both the Void Dominion and the remnants of the Free Collective. Torin's latent connection to Synth Flux, far stronger than he imagined, manifested during a Phantom Guard attack that claimed his family.

Guided by the Collective operative Zyra Kaelen and the smuggler Kael Draven, Torin eventually found Master Zyphos on the swamp world of Mirelos. The ancient Vexari taught him to harness the Radiant Flux, though the training was cut short by Overlord Mordaxis's trap. In the skies above Verdanthis, Torin confronted Xarn Velgor — learning the devastating truth of his parentage.

Rather than succumbing to the Shadow Flux as Mordaxis intended, Torin drew upon his bonds of loyalty and love. He helped lead the assault that destroyed the Void Core, and after Overlord Mordaxis's defeat, worked to restore the Order of Vexari. Torin became a beacon of the Radiant Flux, training a new generation of Vexari Sentinels and proving that hope endures even in the galaxy's darkest era.
