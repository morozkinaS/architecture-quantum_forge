KAEL DRAVEN - SMUGGLER, CAPTAIN OF THE CRIMSON VAGRANT

Kael Draven built his reputation as one of the most skilled pilots in the Remote Reaches territories. Operating from the lawless ports of Dustara and the criminal underworld of Solara, he captained the Crimson Vagrant — a heavily modified freighter that had passed through the hands of numerous smugglers before him. With his Gronthari companion Grothnar at his side, Draven made his living running cargo for syndicates, evading Void Dominion patrols, and betting on his own impossible odds.

Despite his professed neutrality, Draven's path repeatedly intersected with the Free Collective. He initially transported Zyra Kaelen and the Void Core schematics to safety — a job he took purely for payment. Yet repeated encounters with the suffering caused by the Void Dominion eroded his cynicism. His friendship with Torin Valaris and his growing bond with the Collective operatives drew him deeper into the cause.

Draven's tactical brilliance as a pilot proved decisive at the Battle of the Void Core, where he navigated the Crimson Vagrant through the station's trench defenses while under fire from Phantom Guard Star Lances. His signature pulse gun and quick draw became legendary among both allies and enemies.

After the fall of the Void Dominion, Draven continued to serve the Free Collective as a scout and intelligence operative. His intimate knowledge of smuggling routes, criminal networks, and uncharted space made him invaluable. Alongside Grothnar, he explored regions of the galaxy that no chart had recorded, driven by the same restless curiosity that had defined his entire life — though now tempered with purpose.
