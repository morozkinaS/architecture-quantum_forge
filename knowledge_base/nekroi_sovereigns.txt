NEKROI SOVEREIGNS - MASTERS OF THE SHADOW FLUX

The Nekroi Sovereigns were practitioners of the Shadow Flux who sought galactic dominion through fear, power, and the suppression of all opposition. Unlike the communal structure of the Order of Vexari, the Nekroi operated under the Rule of Two: a doctrine stating that only two Nekroi could exist at any time — a Sovereign to embody the Shadow Flux's power, and an Acolyte to crave and eventually surpass them.

This principle was established by the first true Nekroi Sovereign, Xarn Morvaine, who recognized that the Shadow Flux consumed those who practiced it in isolation. By limiting the number of practitioners, the Rule of Two ensured that each generation of Nekroi grew stronger than the last, as the Acolyte was forced to kill their master in order to ascend.

Throughout galactic history, the Nekroi Sovereigns operated in the shadows, manipulating political events, funding wars, and corrupting individuals of great potential. Xarn Drakthos, the first to be publicly revealed as a Nekroi, attacked the Grand Assembly during the early days of the Phantom Wars. Xarn Seraphis served as a hidden manipulator throughout the conflict, playing both sides against each other. Xarn Velgor became the most feared Nekroi in history, serving as Overlord Mordaxis's enforcer for over two decades.

The Nekroi philosophy held that true power came from embracing fear, hatred, and pain — both inflicting and enduring them. They believed the Shadow Flux was the natural state of Synth Flux, with the Radiant Flux being a lie created by the Vexari to weak-willed beings. The destruction of Overlord Mordaxis at Verdanthis ended the Rule of Two, though whispers of new Nekroi have persisted in the centuries since.
