VOID SHARDS - CRYSTALS OF SYNTH FLUX POWER

Void shards were naturally occurring crystalline formations that resonated with the Synth Flux energy field, serving as amplifiers and conduits for the mystical energy. Found deep within planetary crusts on worlds with strong Synth Flux concentrations, void shards were among the most valuable and sought-after materials in the galaxy due to their ability to store, focus, and project Synth Flux energy.

The crystals varied in color, clarity, and power output depending on their formation environment and the specific Synth Flux energy they absorbed during crystallization. Clear, light-refracting void shards aligned with the Radiant Flux and were used by the Order of Vexari for construction of flux blades and meditation aids. Void shards that had been exposed to the Shadow Flux developed a distinctive crimson hue and emitted a darker energy signature, making them ideal for Nekroi weapons.

The primary use of void shards was in the construction of flux blades — the signature weapon of Vexari and Nekroi alike. Each flux blade contained a single void shard at its core, which determined the weapon's color, power, and compatibility with its wielder. A void shard that resonated strongly with a practitioner's flux cells produced a blade of exceptional power, while a poor match resulted in an unreliable or dangerous weapon.

Beyond weapons, void shards served critical technological functions. The Void Core superweapon's primary weapon relied on thousands of void shards to focus and amplify its destructive energy beam. Flux drive systems used void shards to regulate faster-than-light travel, and communication arrays employed them to transmit messages across galactic distances. The scarcity of high-quality void shards made control of their source worlds a strategic priority for both the Void Dominion and the Free Collective during the war.
