FREE COLLECTIVE - RESISTANCE AGAINST TYRANNY

The Free Collective was the organized resistance movement that opposed the Void Dominion throughout its reign. Founded in the aftermath of Directive 66 and the destruction of the Order of Vexari, the Collective drew together military officers, politicians, academics, smugglers, and ordinary citizens who refused to submit to Overlord Mordaxis's tyranny.

The organization was led primarily by Zyra Kaelen, who served as both political leader and strategic commander. Operating from hidden bases on worlds such as Cryos and Verdanthis, the Collective maintained a decentralized structure to resist Void Dominion infiltration. Key leaders communicated through encrypted channels and face-to-face meetings, with no single point of failure that could decapitate the organization.

The Collective's military forces included former Grand Assembly naval personnel who had defected with their ships, Vexari Survivors who continued to fight using their Synth Flux abilities, and ordinary citizens who took up arms against the Phantom Guard. Their most notable assets included the Star Lance fighter squadrons, which conducted guerrilla strikes against Dominion supply lines and military installations.

Despite chronic shortages of personnel, equipment, and resources, the Free Collective achieved several critical victories. Their intelligence operatives secured the Void Core schematics, revealing the superweapon's vulnerability. Their pilots — including Torin Valaris and Kael Draven — delivered the decisive blow at the Battle of Verdanthis. After the fall of the Void Dominion, the Free Collective transitioned into a provisional government, eventually reforming the Grand Assembly and establishing a new democratic order. The Collective remains a powerful symbol of resistance against oppression throughout the galaxy.
