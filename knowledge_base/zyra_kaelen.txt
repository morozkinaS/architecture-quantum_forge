ZYRA KAELEN - PRINCESS OF LYSERATH AND LEADER OF THE FREE COLLECTIVE

Zyra Kaelen was born into the royal house of Lyserath, a serene and culturally rich world renowned for its commitment to art, philosophy, and peaceful diplomacy. As a child of the Lyserathian monarchy, she was educated in statecraft, languages, and the ancient traditions of her people — skills that would prove invaluable in the war to come.

When Overlord Mordaxis rose to power and declared the formation of the Void Dominion, Zyra Kaelen was among the first to recognize the existential threat it posed to galactic freedom. She secretly funneled resources and intelligence to resistance cells, eventually becoming one of the founding architects of the Free Collective. Her political acumen and unshakeable resolve made her the de facto leader of the rebellion, coordinating operations across dozens of star systems.

Zyra Kaelen's personal courage was demonstrated repeatedly: she endured interrogation by Xarn Velgor during the Fall of Lyserath, escaped the destruction of her homeworld's outpost on Nexus Prime, and personally piloted a Star Lance during the decisive Battle of the Void Core. Her leadership during these crises inspired countless beings across the galaxy.

Beyond her military and political role, Zyra Kaelen was a practitioner of Synth Flux, though her training was informal and self-taught. Her sensitivity to the Radiant Flux gave her preternatural instincts and a formidable presence that could unsettle even Nekroi Sovereigns. After the war, she led the transition to democratic governance within the Grand Assembly, ensuring the Void Dominion could never rise again.
