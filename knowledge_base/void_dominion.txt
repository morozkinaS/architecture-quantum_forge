VOID DOMINION - THE AUTHORITARIAN GALACTIC STATE

The Void Dominion was the authoritarian regime that ruled the galaxy for approximately two decades following the Phantom Wars. Established by Overlord Mordaxis after the dissolution of the Grand Assembly, the Dominion replaced democratic governance with absolute imperial rule, enforced by the military might of the Phantom Guard and the terrifying power of the Void Core superweapon.

The Dominion's political structure was absolute: Overlord Mordaxis held supreme authority, with a council of military governors and bureaucratic administrators executing his will across the galaxy. Regional governors, appointed by Mordaxis directly, oversaw sectors of space with broad discretionary powers. Dissent was crushed immediately, with suspected Free Collective sympathizers subjected to interrogation, imprisonment, or public execution.

Militarily, the Void Dominion maintained the largest standing army in galactic history. Millions of Phantom Guard troops occupied every major world, supported by thousands of Star Lance squadrons and a vast naval fleet. The Void Core served as both superweapon and mobile command center, capable of projecting overwhelming force to any system. The Dominion's doctrine relied on terror — the knowledge that any world harboring resistance could be annihilated.

Despite its military dominance, the Void Dominion faced persistent resistance from the Free Collective, which operated through decentralized cells across the galaxy. The Dominion's reliance on fear rather than genuine loyalty created a brittle structure — once the Void Core was destroyed and Overlord Mordaxis defeated at Verdanthis, the regime rapidly collapsed. Many of its former officials and military officers sought amnesty or fled to the galaxy's margins, while the reformed Grand Assembly worked to restore democratic governance.
