CRYOS - THE FROZEN WORLD

Cryos was an ice planet located in the Remote Reaches, characterized by its brutal climate, vast frozen tundra, and perpetual blizzards that could strip flesh from bone within minutes. Despite its inhospitable surface, Cryos became one of the most strategically important worlds during the war against the Void Dominion when the Free Collective established Echo Base — a hidden military installation carved into the planet's ice caves.

The choice of Cryos as a base of operations was driven by its remoteness and the difficulty of locating the base beneath the planet's electromagnetic interference and constant storm cover. The Collective used Cryos as a staging ground for operations in the Remote Reaches, launching Star Lance raids against Dominion supply convoys and gathering intelligence on Void Core movements.

The base's existence was betrayed to the Void Dominion, likely through a tracking beacon planted aboard the Crimson Vagrant during Kael Draven's earlier smuggling operations. Phantom Guard forces, supported by massive ground assault vehicles, attacked the base in a devastating assault that forced the Collective into a hasty evacuation. The Battle of Cryos became a costly defeat for the Collective, though the bulk of their personnel and assets escaped through a secret ice tunnel network.

The evacuation from Cryos was marked by several acts of extraordinary bravery. Master Zyphos, the ancient Vexari mentor, delayed the advancing Phantom Guard to allow the last transports to escape, using his mastery of Synth Flux to hold the corridor alone. The loss of Cryos as a base was a significant strategic setback, but the battle demonstrated the Collective's resilience and willingness to sacrifice for the greater cause.
